# 🕉 Verse 1 — நமச்சிவாய வாழ்க  
### 🌺 Namasivaya Vazhga — The Great Benediction

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
நமச்சிவாய வாழ்க!  
நாதன் தாள் வாழ்க!  
இமைப்பொழுதும் என்நெஞ்சில் நீங்காதான் தாள் வாழ்க!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_namassivāya vāzhga!_  
_nāthan tāl vāzhga!_  
_imaippozhudum en nenjil nīngādhān tāl vāzhga!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**  

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| நமச்சிவாய | The holy Panchāksharam (Na-Ma-Si-Vā-Ya); salutations to Śiva |
| வாழ்க | Let it live! Let it flourish! |
| நாடன் | The Lord, Master; Śiva as the supreme refuge |
| தாள் | Feet — symbol of surrender and liberation |
| இமைப்பொழுதும் | Even for the blink of an eye |
| என் நெஞ்சில் | In my heart |
| நீங்காதான் | The One who never departs |
| தாள் | His divine feet |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**  
“May the sacred name *Namassivāya* flourish!  
May the feet of the Lord flourish!  
May the feet of the One who never leaves my heart —  
not even for a blink — flourish forever!”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**  

⭐ **1. “Namasivaya Vazhga” — Let the Name Live in Me**  
The hymn opens not by asking for blessings, but by blessing the Divine Name itself —  
a profound act of devotion where the devotee’s identity dissolves into the mantra.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let my breath itself become Namasivaya.  
> Every inhale: Nama — surrender.  
> Every exhale: Śivāya — union with Śiva.”

⭐ **2. “Nadhan Thaal Vazhga” — The Feet as Refuge**  
The divine feet symbolize:  
- Surrender  
- Shelter  
- Dissolution of ego  
- Entry into grace  

> *Meditation:*  
> “I place my burdens at the Lord’s feet and walk in freedom.”

⭐ **3. “Imaippozhudum En Nenjil Neengādhaan” — The Ever-Present One**  
Śiva is described as the One who resides permanently within the heart.  
Not even for the blinking of an eye does He abandon the devotee.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Śiva is not outside — He is the unbroken awareness within me.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**  
This verse is the essence of Śaiva Siddhānta:  
- the mantra,  
- the Lord,  
- the feet of refuge,  
- the indwelling presence,  
- the inseparability of God and devotee.

A perfect opening to Sivapuranam.

# 🕉 Verse 2 — உளன்று திரியாதே  
### 🌺 Ulanru Thiriyadhe — The Seeking Mind

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
உளன்று திரியாதே  
ஓர்கனெஞ் சினையே!  
அளந்தான் அடியடி யார்க்கு அருள்செய்தான் ஞானமே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_uḷanṟu thiriyādē_  
_ōrga neñjinaiyē!_  
_aḷandhān aḍiyaḍiyārkku aruḷ seidān ñānamē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| உளன்று | Wandering inward helplessly; confused within |
| திரியாதே | Do not roam, do not wander |
| ஓர்க | Reflect, contemplate |
| நெஞ்சினையே | O my heart! |
| அளந்தான் | The One who measured the worlds (Vāmana / Śiva as cosmic measurer) |
| அடியடி யார்க்கு | To the humble devotees |
| அருள் செய்தான் | He grants grace |
| ஞானமே | This itself is true wisdom |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“O my heart, do not wander in confusion.  
Reflect instead!  
For He who measured the worlds  
bestows His grace upon His devotees —  
and that grace *is* the highest wisdom.”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. “Do Not Wander, O Heart”**  
The mind moves outward, chasing names, forms, desires.  
But the verse is instruction: *Stop. Turn inward. Reflect.*

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let my heart rest from wandering and return to awareness.”

⭐ **2. The Lord Who Measured the Worlds**  
This hints at the cosmic Śiva:  
the One whose presence permeates all three realms —  
and yet, He is accessible to the simplest devotee.

> *Meditation:*  
> “The Infinite bows toward the surrendered heart.”

⭐ **3. Grace as Wisdom**  
Not intellectual study, not debate —  
**Grace itself is Jñāna.**  
When the Lord’s compassion touches the seeker,  
clarity dawns effortlessly.

> *Meditation:*  
> “May grace awaken the wisdom already within me.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**  
The verse teaches the core of Śivānubhava:  
- still the mind,  
- contemplate the Divine,  
- receive grace,  
- awaken into wisdom.

This shift from wandering → reflection → grace → wisdom  
is the entire spiritual path summarized in a single verse.

# 🕉 Verse 3 — எந்நாளும் நின்னடியார்  
### 🌺 Ennaalum Ninnadiyār — Eternal Devotion

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
எந்நாளும் நின்னடியார்  
கண்ணில் மலர் தவமோரார்  
மன்னாவே மன்னாவே! வாழ்கின்றார் மன்னாவே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_ennālum ninn-aḍiyār_  
_kaṇṇil malar thavam ōrār_  
_mannāvē mannāvē! vāzhginrār mannāvē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| எந்நாளும் | Forever, at all times |
| நின் அடியார் | Your devotees; those at Your sacred feet |
| கண்ணில் மலர் | Flowers in the eyes — i.e., filled with devotion and tenderness |
| தவம் ஓரார் | Those performing tapas (austerity / deep devotion) |
| மன்னாவே | O Eternal One! O Sovereign Lord! |
| வாழ்கின்றார் | They live, they flourish |
| மன்னாவே | Again addressing the Imperishable Lord |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“Forever, Your devotees  
hold flowers of devotion in their eyes  
and perform their inner tapas.  
O Eternal One, O Sovereign Lord,  
it is they who truly live and flourish!”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. “Flowers in the Eyes”**  
A poetic way of saying:  
Their gaze is softened by devotion,  
their hearts bloom with purity.

> *Meditation:*  
> “May my seeing of the world be softened by devotion.”

⭐ **2. Tapas as Inner Devotion**  
Tapas is not external hardship.  
It is *steadfast remembrance* of Śiva.

> *Meditation:*  
> “My constant remembrance of the Divine is my true tapas.”

⭐ **3. “It Is They Who Truly Live”**  
Those who live in awareness of Śiva  
have tasted the fullness of life —  
others merely exist.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Life without devotion is survival; life with devotion is liberation.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**  
The verse describes the true devotee:  
- eyes blooming with love,  
- mind absorbed in tapas,  
- heart anchored in Śiva,  
- life flourishing inwardly.

It tells us:  
**Devotion is not a part of life —  
it *is* the essence of life.**

# 🕉 Verse 4 — நின்னடியார் தம் பிரிந்தார்  
### 🌺 Ninnadiyār Tham Pirindhār — The Pain of Separation from Śiva

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
நின்னடியார் தம் பிரிந்தார்  
நெஞ்சம் விழுந்தது போல  
என்னடி யேன் உயிர் நீங்கும்  
இருக்கின்ற தென்னடியே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_ninn-aḍiyār tham pirindhār_  
_neñjam vizhundhadu pōla_  
_ennadi yēn uyir nīngum_  
_irukkinradhu ennadiyē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| நின் அடியார் | Your devotees |
| தம் பிரிந்தார் | Who have parted / separated |
| நெஞ்சம் விழுந்தது போல | As though the heart has fallen / collapsed |
| என்னடி யேன் | O me! What am I to do? |
| உயிர் நீங்கும் | My life departs, breath leaves me |
| இருக்கின்றது | What remains / what exists |
| என்னடியே | Only me, helpless |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“When Your devotees part from me,  
my heart collapses as if it has fallen.  
O what shall I do?  
My very life seems to leave me —  
and only this helpless self remains.”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. Devotees as Life-Breath**  
This verse does not lament separation from Śiva,  
but from **Śiva’s devotees**.  
Why?  
Because the devotees carry the fragrance of Śiva’s presence.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Company of the holy is the nearness of God.”

⭐ **2. The Heart That Cannot Bear Separation**  
In true devotion, loss of divine company feels like  
the collapse of the inner temple.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let my heart stay rooted in those who guide me toward Śiva.”

⭐ **3. The Cry of the Helpless Soul**  
The verse reveals intense bhakti:  
the feeling that without divine companionship,  
life itself drains away.

> *Meditation:*  
> “When I drift from the Divine path,  
> may I feel the longing that pulls me back.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse teaches:  
- The devotees of Śiva are not ordinary people;  
  they are carriers of divine light.  
- To lose their company is to lose spiritual direction.  
- The sincere devotee yearns for the holy company  
  as much as for Śiva Himself.

It echoes the ancient truth:  

**“To love Śiva is to love His devotees.”**

# 🕉 Verse 5 — அடியார் திருவடிக் கீழ்  
### 🌺 Adiyār Thiruvadik Keezh — Shelter Beneath the Feet of Devotees

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
அடியார் திருவடிக் கீழ்  
அமர்ந்தேன் அடைந்தேன் என்பேன்  
கடியார் அகத்தகமும்  
கலங்காது காப்பரனே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_aḍiyār thiruvadi-k-kīzh_  
_amarnthen adaindhen enbēn_  
_kadiyār agaththagamum_  
_kalaṅgādhū kāpparanē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| அடியார் | Devotees of Śiva |
| திருவடி கீழ் | Beneath their sacred feet; under their protection |
| அமர்ந்தேன் | I have seated myself / settled |
| அடைந்தேன் | I have reached / taken refuge |
| என்பேன் | I will say / I declare |
| கடியார் | Those who cause harm / enemies |
| அகத்தகம் | Internal impurities, negativities |
| கலங்காது | Without disturbance or fear |
| காப்பரனே | O Protector! Guard me! |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“I declare that I have seated myself  
beneath the sacred feet of Your devotees.  
O Protector!  
So that neither hostile forces  
nor inner impurities disturb me —  
guard me always!”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. Refuge Under the Feet of Devotees**  
The verse honors **Śiva’s devotees** as the safest and noblest refuge.  
In Tamil Śaiva tradition, serving the devotees is equal to serving Śiva.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let humility before the saints become my fortress.”

⭐ **2. The Inner Enemy: Impurities**  
The “enemies” mentioned are not external foes,  
but internal disturbances — anger, jealousy, pride, confusion.

> *Meditation:*  
> “May Śiva remove the enemies that hide within me.”

⭐ **3. Śiva as the Fearless Protector**  
The devotee expresses unwavering confidence:  
“With Śiva as my guardian,  
*what can disturb me?*”

> *Meditation:*  
> “I place my mind in the hands of the One who guards my being.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse teaches three profound truths:

1. **The devotees of Śiva are living temples.**  
2. **Spiritual danger comes from within, not outside.**  
3. **Śiva protects those who surrender with sincerity.**

Thus, refuge → purity → protection  
forms the sacred triad of Śaiva Siddhānta practice.

# 🕉 Verse 6 — துன்பம் நினைவிலனே  
### 🌺 Dunbam Ninaivilanē — No Sorrow in His Remembrance

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
துன்பம் நினைவிலனே!  
சுகமே நினைவிலனே!  
இனிமேல் எமக்கு இடருண்டு  
இருக்கின்றது என்னிலனே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_dunbam ninaivilanē!_  
_sugamē ninaivilanē!_  
_inimēl emakku idarundu_  
_irukkinrathu ennilanē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| துன்பம் | Sorrow, suffering, hardship |
| நினைவிலனே | Does not arise in remembrance |
| சுகமே | Joy, bliss, comfort |
| இனிமேல் | Hereafter, from now on |
| எமக்கு | For us |
| இடர் | Difficulty, misfortune |
| உண்டு | Exists |
| என்னிலனே | Not for me; it no longer exists for me |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“O Śiva!  
In Your remembrance, sorrow does not exist;  
in Your remembrance, even joy does not exist—  
for beyond both joy and sorrow,  
no misfortune remains for me anymore.”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. Beyond Sorrow and Joy**  
This verse expresses a profound truth:  
Real spiritual awakening is not about replacing sorrow with joy —  
it is **transcending both**.

> *Meditation:*  
> “True peace is beyond the opposites.”

⭐ **2. Remembrance as Liberation**  
When the mind rests on Śiva,  
worldly pains and pleasures lose their grip.  
The devotee shifts from reaction to awareness.

> *Meditation:*  
> “In remembering Him, I rise above what disturbs me.”

⭐ **3. No Misfortune for the One Whose Mind is Anchored**  
Problems may exist,  
but they no longer *define* or *bind* the devotee.  
This is inner freedom.

> *Meditation:*  
> “The outer world may stir, but inner Śiva remains still.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse reveals the essence of Śaiva mysticism:

- Sorrow and joy are states of the mind  
- Remembrance of Śiva transcends both  
- In the presence of Śiva-consciousness, misfortune is powerless  

It is the declaration of a mind that has tasted  
**the peace beyond duality.**

# 🕉 Verse 7 — நின்னடியார் தந்த நன்மை  
### 🌺 Ninnadiyār Thanda Nanmai — The Blessings Given by Śiva’s Devotees

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
நின்னடியார் தந்த நன்மை  
நினைந்து நினைந்து உணர்ந்தேன்  
என்னடியே! உலகில் யார்க்கும்  
இத்தகையே இன்பமோ?
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_ninn-aḍiyār thanda nanmai_  
_ninaindhu ninaindhu uṇarnthēn_  
_ennadiyē! ulagil yārkkum_  
_iththagayē inbamō?_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| நின் அடியார் | Your devotees |
| தந்த நன்மை | The goodness/blessing they have given |
| நினைந்து நினைந்து | Thinking again and again; deeply contemplating |
| உணர்ந்தேன் | I realized; I understood |
| என்னடியே | Oh dear self! Oh my soul! |
| உலகில் | In this world |
| யார்க்கும் | To anyone |
| இத்தகையே | Such as this; of this nature |
| இன்பமோ | Is there any joy like this? |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“I reflect again and again  
on the blessings that Your devotees have given me,  
and I realize, O my self,  
that in all this world  
there is no joy equal to this!”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. Blessings Come Through Devotees**  
Śiva works through His devotees —  
their presence, words, guidance, and compassion  
become channels of divine upliftment.

> *Meditation:*  
> “The company of the holy is a gift from Śiva Himself.”

⭐ **2. The Joy of Spiritual Companionship**  
Worldly joys fade;  
the joy arising from divine association  
keeps growing and deepening.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let me value the people who turn my mind toward the Divine.”

⭐ **3. Self-Reflection as Awakening**  
The devotee speaks to his own heart:  
“When I contemplate the grace I received,  
how can I deny it?  
How can anything worldly compare?”

> *Meditation:*  
> “Awareness of grace is itself grace.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse teaches:

- The highest gifts in life come not from wealth or status  
- but from **contact with enlightened beings and true devotees**  
- who awaken in us a deeper longing and understanding of Śiva.

Such joy — **inner joy** — cannot be matched by any worldly pleasure.

The verse celebrates the transformative power of spiritual friendship  
known in Tamil Śaivism as **tiru-tōṇḍar nilai** —  
“the blessed state of belonging to the devotees of Śiva.”

# 🕉 Verse 8 — உள்ளத்தினின் றொருவன் உரை  
### 🌺 Ullaththinin Oruvan Urai — The Inner Voice of Śiva

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
உள்ளத்தினின் றொருவன் உரை  
உயிரின் உள்ளே உரைப்பான்  
தெள்ளத் தெளிந்து திகழும்  
சிவலோகத்திற் செல்லுவனே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_uḷlaththinil oruvan urai_  
_uyirin uḷlē uraippān_  
_theḷḷath theḷindhu thigaḻum_  
_sivalōgaththil chelluvanē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| உள்ளத்தினில் | In the heart, within the mind |
| ஒருவன் | The One — Śiva |
| உரை | Speaks, instructs, reveals |
| உயிரின் உள்ளே | Deep inside the living soul |
| உரைப்பான் | He will speak, guide inwardly |
| தெள்ளத் தெளிந்து | Clearly, luminously, without doubt |
| திகழும் | Shining, radiating |
| சிவலோகத்திற்கு | To the world of Śiva; the state of liberation |
| செல்லுவனே | You will go, you will reach |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“The One within the heart  
speaks from deep inside the soul.  
When His voice is heard clearly,  
you will shine in clarity  
and reach the world of Śiva.”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. Śiva Speaks Within**  
This is one of the most powerful declarations of Sivapuranam:  
Śiva is not distant — He speaks from **within**.  
The Guru, the scriptures, and the inner voice are all expressions of Him.

> *Meditation:*  
> “The guidance I seek outside is whispering inside me.”

⭐ **2. When the Inner Voice Clears, Doubt Dissolves**  
The phrase *teḷḷath theḷindhu* means  
“absolutely clear, like a crystal without blemish.”  
When Śiva’s presence becomes clear within,  
confusion disappears.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Clarity is the light of Śiva shining in my mind.”

⭐ **3. The Path to Śivalōka is Inner Illumination**  
Here, ‘Śivalōka’ does not only mean a heavenly abode.  
It means  
- freedom,  
- inner radiance,  
- union with Śiva-consciousness.

> *Meditation:*  
> “I travel inward, toward the world of Śiva.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse teaches the deepest spiritual truth:  
**Śiva is the Inner Guru**,  
the silent guide who reveals Himself through intuition,  
insight, clarity, and luminous awareness.

When the heart becomes quiet,  
His voice becomes unmistakably clear.

This verse is the heart of Tamil Śaiva mysticism:  
> “To know oneself is to hear the voice of Śiva.”

# 🕉 Verse 9 — எங்குமுள்ளாய் என்னுள்ளாய்

```tamil
எங்குமுள்ளாய் என்னுள்ளாய்  
எனைவிட்டு நீங்காதே  
செங்கமலத்து உள் கிடந்தாய்
```

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

|------|---------|

| நீங்காதே | Do not depart; never leave |
| செங்கமலத்து உள் | Inside the red lotus (symbol of the heart) |

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“You are everywhere;  
You are within me.  
Do not ever leave me.  
You dwell in the red lotus of my heart —  
O abundant Divine Grace!”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. Omnipresence and Indwelling Presence**  
Śiva is both:  
- the vastness permeating the cosmos, and  
- the intimate presence living within the heart.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Where can I go where Śiva is not?”  
> “How can I be without the One who lives within me?”

⭐ **2. “Do Not Leave Me” — The Cry of the Soul**  
Although Śiva never leaves,  
the mind often *feels* separation due to ignorance.  
This prayer is not to Śiva —  
it is to one’s own wavering mind.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let my mind never wander away from the truth that Śiva is within.”

⭐ **3. The Lotus of the Heart**  
The “red lotus” symbolizes the **spiritual heart (hṛdaya)**,  
the seat of awareness.  
Śiva rests there in radiant stillness.

> *Meditation:*  
> “My heart is the throne where Śiva sits.”

⭐ **4. Infinite Grace as Inner Fullness**  
Divine grace is not something given from outside;  
it flowers from within when the heart becomes pure.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Grace is the overflow of inner presence.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This is one of the most beloved verses of Sivapuranam.  
It encapsulates the entire philosophy of Tamil Śaivism:

- Śiva is cosmic (engum uḷḷāy)  
- Śiva is personal (enn uḷḷāy)  
- Śiva is inseparable from the devotee (ennai viṭṭu nīngādhē)  
- Śiva is the heart-lotus (seng-kamalam)  
- Śiva is boundless grace (thiruvaruḷ sezhumai)

It is both a declaration and a prayer —  
a perfect union of philosophy and devotion.

Instead of asking that his own suffering stop,

- benevolent,

The devotee’s joy becomes universal compassion.  
The devotee’s suffering becomes universal prayer.

This is **pure bhakti**.

> *Meditation:*  
> “May whatever I receive become a blessing for the world.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse is considered one of the crowning jewels of *Thiruvasagam*.

It reveals:
- the heart of a realized devotee,  
- the compassion of a jīvanmukta (liberated soul),  
- the love of Śiva toward all beings.

It proclaims the highest truth:

> **“My liberation is incomplete unless the whole world is blessed.”**

Saint Manikkavasagar stands here as the embodiment of  
pure selflessness, pure devotion, and universal love.

- the transcendence of Śiva

- His intimacy

This verse transforms the devotee’s heart into  
**a temple overflowing with ecstatic love.**

- Śiva is Awareness

- Śiva is All

- Śiva is the Divine Mother  

A single verse holds the entire theology of Tamil Śaivism,  
binding philosophy with deep personal devotion.

⭐ **5. Śiva as the Manifest Being**  
Śiva is “this” (ivan) —  
the world we experience.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Every being before me is Śiva in disguise.”

⭐ **6. Śiva Beyond All Limits**  
Even though He appears as many forms,  
He transcends all forms,  
all categories,  
all boundaries.

> *Meditation:*  
> “He is the Infinite in which all boundaries dissolve.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse is a declaration of **absolute non-duality** wrapped in devotion:

- Śiva is love  
- Śiva is grace  
- Śiva is the jīva  
- Śiva is ignorance  
- Śiva is the world  
- Śiva is the transcendent beyond all these  

No line in Sivapuranam expresses Advaita so beautifully:

> **“You are everything, even the things I thought were not You.”**

This is the heart of Manikkavacagar’s realization:  
**All is Śiva, and Śiva is all.**

- “You alone exist.”  
- “You are the imperishable nectar of bliss.”  
- “You are the Reality, the Life, the Awareness.”

It is one of the purest statements of monistic Śaiva Siddhānta,  
and yet drenched in devotion.

In a single verse, Manikkavacagar expresses  
the entire truth of the Self:

> **Śiva alone is — as Love, Life, Light, and Being.**

not just the universe, but time, space, and consciousness itself.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Every moment of my existence begins in Śiva.”

---

⭐ **2. Śiva as Life (Uyir)**  
He is the breath, the heartbeat, the prāṇa.  
Life is not *given* by Śiva — it **is** Śiva.

> *Meditation:*  
> “The life in me is Śiva living in me.”

---

⭐ **3. Śiva as the Path (Kathiyāgu)**  
He is not only the goal but also the way.  
Surrender to Him is both journey and destination.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Walking toward Śiva is walking with Śiva.”

---

⭐ **4. Cause of All Causes (Kāraṇa Nāyagan)**  
Śiva is not an effect;  
He is the primordial ground from which all arises.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Behind every cause, there is Śiva.”

---

⭐ **5. Śiva as Form and Formless**  
This verse continues the non-dual revelation:

- **Form** → His grace made visible  
- **Formless** → His infinite presence  

> *Meditation:*  
> “He appears as form so I may love Him;  
> He is formless so I may become Him.”

---

⭐ **6. Śiva as OM (Oṅkāra)**  
OM is not a symbol — it is Śiva’s very vibration,  
the sound-body of the cosmos.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Every vibration in the universe is Śiva chanting OM.”

---

⭐ **7. Eternal Radiance in the Heart**  
Śiva does not visit the heart —  
He **resides there**, shining forever.

> *Meditation:*  
> “In the silent center of my heart, Śiva glows.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

This verse reveals the full map of spiritual reality:

- **Cause** → Śiva  
- **Life** → Śiva  
- **Path** → Śiva  
- **Goal** → Śiva  
- **Form** → Śiva  
- **Formless** → Śiva  
- **Cosmic OM** → Śiva  
- **Inner Light** → Śiva  

Manikkavacagar is not describing theology —  
he is describing **direct mystical experience**.

In this verse, all duality collapses into one truth:

> **Śiva alone exists — as cause, life, path, universe, and the light within.**

# 🕉 Verse 17 — அறுவாய் அருவாய் அருவாய்க்  
### 🌺 Aṟuvāy Aruvāy Aruvāyk — You Are the Six, the Formless, and Beyond Formlessness

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
அறுவாய் அருவாய் அருவாய்க்  
காணப் படாததுவாய்  
பெறுவாய் பெறாமை யாகிப்  
பிறப்பின்றிப் பேரின்பமே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_aṟuvāy aruvāy aruvāyk_  
_kāṇa ppadādhadhuvāy_  
_peṟuvāy peṟāmai yāgip_  
_piṟappinrip pēr-inbamē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| அறுவாய் | As the six; the six acts, six chakras, or six divine faces |
| அருவாய் | As formless |
| அருவாய்க் | As that which transcends even the formless |
| காணப் படாதது | Unseeable; beyond perception |
| பெறுவாய் | You are attainment |
| பெறாமை | You are non-attainment |
| ஆகிப் | Becoming; expressing as |
| பிறப்பின்றி | Unborn; without origin |
| பேரின்பமே | Supreme, boundless bliss |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“You are the Six,  
You are the formless,  
and the One beyond even the formless — unseen by any eye.  

You are attainment and non-attainment,  
the unborn,  
and the Supreme Bliss.”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. Śiva as the Six (Aṟu)**  
Represents all spiritual evolution — the six acts, chakras, and divine expressions.

⭐ **2. Śiva as Formless (Aru)**  
Subtle, ungraspable, untouched by senses.

⭐ **3. Śiva Beyond the Formless**  
Transcending even abstraction — the Absolute.

⭐ **4. Śiva as Attainment & Non-Attainment**  
He is moksha, and the dissolution of the seeker.

⭐ **5. Śiva as Unborn**  
Timeless, beginningless awareness.

⭐ **6. Śiva as Supreme Bliss**  
Ecstasy beyond duality — pēr-inbam.

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**

Śiva is evolution and transcendence, form and formlessness,  
the seeker, the liberation, and the bliss beyond liberation.
