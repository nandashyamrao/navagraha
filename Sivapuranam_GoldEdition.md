# 🕉 Verse 1 — நமச்சிவாய வாழ்க  
### 🌺 Namasivaya Vazhga — The Great Benediction

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
நமச்சிவாய வாழ்க!  
நாதன் தாள் வாழ்க!  
இமைப்பொழுதும் என்நெஞ்சில் நீங்காதான் தாள் வாழ்க!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_namassivāya vāzhga!_  
_nāthan tāl vāzhga!_  
_imaippozhudum en nenjil nīngādhān tāl vāzhga!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**  

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| நமச்சிவாய | The holy Panchāksharam (Na-Ma-Si-Vā-Ya); salutations to Śiva |
| வாழ்க | Let it live! Let it flourish! |
| நாடன் | The Lord, Master; Śiva as the supreme refuge |
| தாள் | Feet — symbol of surrender and liberation |
| இமைப்பொழுதும் | Even for the blink of an eye |
| என் நெஞ்சில் | In my heart |
| நீங்காதான் | The One who never departs |
| தாள் | His divine feet |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**  
“May the sacred name *Namassivāya* flourish!  
May the feet of the Lord flourish!  
May the feet of the One who never leaves my heart —  
not even for a blink — flourish forever!”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**  

⭐ **1. “Namasivaya Vazhga” — Let the Name Live in Me**  
The hymn opens not by asking for blessings, but by blessing the Divine Name itself —  
a profound act of devotion where the devotee’s identity dissolves into the mantra.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let my breath itself become Namasivaya.  
> Every inhale: Nama — surrender.  
> Every exhale: Śivāya — union with Śiva.”

⭐ **2. “Nadhan Thaal Vazhga” — The Feet as Refuge**  
The divine feet symbolize:  
- Surrender  
- Shelter  
- Dissolution of ego  
- Entry into grace  

> *Meditation:*  
> “I place my burdens at the Lord’s feet and walk in freedom.”

⭐ **3. “Imaippozhudum En Nenjil Neengādhaan” — The Ever-Present One**  
Śiva is described as the One who resides permanently within the heart.  
Not even for the blinking of an eye does He abandon the devotee.

> *Meditation:*  
> “Śiva is not outside — He is the unbroken awareness within me.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**  
This verse is the essence of Śaiva Siddhānta:  
- the mantra,  
- the Lord,  
- the feet of refuge,  
- the indwelling presence,  
- the inseparability of God and devotee.

A perfect opening to Sivapuranam.

# 🕉 Verse 2 — உளன்று திரியாதே  
### 🌺 Ulanru Thiriyadhe — The Seeking Mind

---

🔱 **Tamil (Original Verse)**  
```tamil
உளன்று திரியாதே  
ஓர்கனெஞ் சினையே!  
அளந்தான் அடியடி யார்க்கு அருள்செய்தான் ஞானமே!
```

🔤 **Transliteration**  
_uḷanṟu thiriyādē_  
_ōrga neñjinaiyē!_  
_aḷandhān aḍiyaḍiyārkku aruḷ seidān ñānamē!_

---

🧩 **Compound Breakdown**

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| உளன்று | Wandering inward helplessly; confused within |
| திரியாதே | Do not roam, do not wander |
| ஓர்க | Reflect, contemplate |
| நெஞ்சினையே | O my heart! |
| அளந்தான் | The One who measured the worlds (Vāmana / Śiva as cosmic measurer) |
| அடியடி யார்க்கு | To the humble devotees |
| அருள் செய்தான் | He grants grace |
| ஞானமே | This itself is true wisdom |

---

🌿 **Simple Translation**

“O my heart, do not wander in confusion.  
Reflect instead!  
For He who measured the worlds  
bestows His grace upon His devotees —  
and that grace *is* the highest wisdom.”

---

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1. “Do Not Wander, O Heart”**  
The mind moves outward, chasing names, forms, desires.  
But the verse is instruction: *Stop. Turn inward. Reflect.*

> *Meditation:*  
> “Let my heart rest from wandering and return to awareness.”

⭐ **2. The Lord Who Measured the Worlds**  
This hints at the cosmic Śiva:  
the One whose presence permeates all three realms —  
and yet, He is accessible to the simplest devotee.

> *Meditation:*  
> “The Infinite bows toward the surrendered heart.”

⭐ **3. Grace as Wisdom**  
Not intellectual study, not debate —  
**Grace itself is Jñāna.**  
When the Lord’s compassion touches the seeker,  
clarity dawns effortlessly.

> *Meditation:*  
> “May grace awaken the wisdom already within me.”

---

✨ **Golden Reflection**  
The verse teaches the core of Śivānubhava:  
- still the mind,  
- contemplate the Divine,  
- receive grace,  
- awaken into wisdom.

This shift from wandering → reflection → grace → wisdom  
is the entire spiritual path summarized in a single verse.
