>

---

## 🔱 Philosophical Significance (Upaniṣadic Core)

This is **pure Advaita Vedānta**, declared **without symbolism**:

| Statement | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pratyakṣaṁ tattvam asi | Truth is not distant — it is immediately present |
| Kartā–Dhartā–Hartā | Creation–Preservation–Dissolution unified |
| Sarvaṁ brahma | Entire universe is non-dual Brahman |
| Sākṣād ātmā | Gaṇapati = Inner Self |

➡️ **Gaṇapati is not a deity “outside” — He is Consciousness itself.**

- the law that governs the universe
