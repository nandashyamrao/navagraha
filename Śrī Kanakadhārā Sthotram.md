🕉️ **Śrī Kanakadhārā Stotram**
===============================


📖 **About This Hymn**  
The _Kanakadhārā Stotram_ was composed by **Śrī Ādi Śaṅkarācārya** out of pure compassion, to bless a poor Brahmin woman who had nothing to give except one dry amla fruit. Moved by her sincerity, Śaṅkara recited these 22 verses invoking **Mahā Lakṣmī**, the Goddess of prosperity. Legend says that immediately _golden amlas_ showered upon her home.

🧘‍♀️ **Deeper Meditation Meaning (Condensed Practice)**  
Sit with a steady, gentle breath; on the inhale recall “Śrī,” on the exhale “Lakṣmī,” letting the mind rest in the heart. Visualize a soft golden rain descending through the crown into the heart, dissolving scarcity into quiet sufficiency. Offer one authentic feeling of lack (like the poor woman’s single āmla) and witness it being transformed into compassionate fullness to be shared outward.


🕉 **Verse 1 — वन्दे वन्दारुमन्दारम्**
======================================

🔱 **Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)**  
वन्दे वन्दारुमन्दारमिन्दिरानन्दकन्दलम् ।  
अमन्दानन्दसन्दोहबन्धुरं सिन्धुराननम् ॥

* * *

🔤 **Transliteration**  
vandē vandāru-mandāram indira-ānanda-kandalam |  
amandānanda-sandōha-bandhuraṁ sindhurānanam ||

* * *

🧩 **Padaccheda & Word-by-Word Meaning**

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| वन्दे | I bow, I adore |
| वन्दारु-मन्दारम् | the heavenly coral-tree (mandāra) for devotees who bow; the one who grants their wishes |
| इन्दिरा-आनन्द-कन्दलम् | the blossoming creeper of Indirā’s (Lakṣmī’s) bliss |
| अमन्द-आनन्द-सन्दोह-बन्धुरम् | abundantly filled with limitless joy; radiantly beautiful |
| सिन्धुर-आननम् | having an elephant-like glorious face (symbol of majesty and auspiciousness) |

* * *

🌿 **Simple Translation**  
“I bow to Her who is the mandāra tree for all devotees—  
the blooming vine of Lakṣmī’s bliss,  
the embodiment of boundless joy,  
and whose radiant face shines with majestic grace  
like the auspicious elephant.”

* * *

🧘 **Meditative Insight**  
⭐ **1\. The Giver of Wish-Fulfilling Grace**  
Calling Her _mandāra_ suggests that for those who approach with faith, She flowers effortlessly with blessings.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Grace blossoms wherever devotion is sincere.”

⭐ **2\. The Source of Lakṣmī’s Bliss**  
Lakṣmī Herself is the ocean of prosperity and well-being. This verse honors the very _source_ of that bliss.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Prosperity is a divine fragrance emerging from inner contentment.”

⭐ **3\. Infinite Joy as Her Nature**  
_Amandānanda_ means joy so great it cannot be diminished.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “True joy arises from my divine center, not from circumstances.”

⭐ **4\. The Auspicious Elephant-Faced Radiance**  
Elephants symbolize wisdom, royalty, and auspicious beginnings.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May auspiciousness guard my path like a mighty elephant.”

🕉 **Verse 2 — अङ्गं हरेः पुलकभूषणम्**
======================================

🔱 **Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)**  
अङ्गं हरेः पुलकभूषणमाश्रयन्ती  
भृङ्गाङ्गनेव मुकुलाभरणं तमालम् ।  
अङ्गीकृताखिलविभूतिरपाङ्गलीला  
माङ्गल्यदाऽस्तु मम मङ्गलदेवतायाः ॥ १॥


* * *

🔤 **Transliteration**  
aṅgaṁ harēḥ pulaka-bhūṣaṇam āśrayantī  
bhṛṅgāṅganēva mukulābharaṇaṁ tamālam |  
aṅgī-kṛtākhila-vibhūtir apāṅga-līlā  
māṅgalyadā ’stu mama maṅgala-dēvatāyāḥ ||

* * *

🧩 **Padaccheda & Word-by-Word Meaning**

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अङ्गम् | the body, limbs |
| हरेः | of Hari (Viṣṇu) |
| पुलक-भूषणम् | adorned with divine thrill/hair standing in ecstasy |
| आश्रयन्ती | approaching, embracing |
| भृङ्ग-अङ्गना-इव | like a female bee |
| मुकुल-अभरणम् | adorned with flower-buds |
| तमालम् | the dark tamāla tree (symbolic of Viṣṇu’s blue body) |
| अङ्गीकृत-अखिल-विभूति: | She who embodies all divine powers and glories |
| अपाङ्ग-लीला | the playful side-glance |
| माङ्गल्यदा अस्तु | may it bestow auspiciousness |
| मम | upon me |
| मङ्गल-देवतायाः | of the Goddess of Auspiciousness (Lakṣmī) |

* * *

🌿 **Simple Translation**  
“May the auspicious Goddess—  
She whose playful sidelong glance embodies all divine powers  
and who clings to the dark, blooming body of Hari  
like a bee to a tamāla tree adorned with buds—  
grant me blessings and auspiciousness.”

* * *

🧘 **Meditative Insight**

⭐ **1\. Divine Ecstasy as Ornament**  
Hari’s form is described as _pulaka-bhūṣaṇa_—hair standing on end from bliss.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Divine joy itself becomes an ornament.”

⭐ **2\. The Bee and the Tamāla Tree**  
Lakṣmī is likened to a bee drawn irresistibly to Viṣṇu, the dark tamāla tree.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Seek the Divine with the natural longing of a bee for fragrance.”

⭐ **3\. Power of the Sidelong Glance**  
Her _apāṅga-līlā_—just a playful side-glance—contains all glories.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Grace can transform an entire life with a single moment of vision.”

⭐ **4\. The Bestower of Auspiciousness**  
She is _maṅgala-devatā_—the very embodiment of auspiciousness.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “When the heart aligns with the Divine, auspiciousness flows effortlessly.”

The bee moves around the lotus the way Her eyes move around Viṣṇu.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let my attention revolve around the Divine, drawn by inner fragrance.”

⭐ **3\. Prosperity as Divine Grace**  
Here _śriyaṁ_ is not material wealth alone but fullness of life.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May I receive the abundance that nurtures the soul.”

⭐ **4\. Born of the Ocean**  
She is _sāgara-sambhavā_—born from the churning of the cosmic ocean.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Blessings rise when inner depths are churned with sincerity.”

🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let my attention be steady and rooted in truth.”

⭐ **4\. Prosperity from Presence**  
Her simple glance becomes _bhūti_—prosperity, fullness, completeness.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Prosperity arises where the Divine is sincerely remembered.”

🕉 **The Kanakadhārā Miracle — Closing Narrative**
==================================================

🔱 **Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)**  
अयं स्तवः स्वामिना शङ्करभगवत्पादेन ब्रह्मव्रतस्थेन कालटिनाम्नि  
स्वग्राम एवाकिञ्चन्यपरिखिन्नाया द्विजगृहिण्या निर्धनत्वमार्जनाय  
निरमायि ।

तेन स्तवेन प्रीता लक्ष्मीर्विप्रं विपुलधनदानेनाप्रीणयत्  
इति शङ्करविजयतः समाधिगम्यते ।

* * *

🌿 **Simple Meaning (Beautiful Narrative Form)**
================================================

When Śrī Ādi Śaṅkara was still a young sannyāsin,  
he went for alms and arrived at the hut of a poor Brahmin woman.  
She had nothing—not even a grain to offer.  
Yet with all her heart, she searched her home  
and found only **one dry āmla fruit**.

With devotion, she placed it before the young monk.

Seeing her sincerity and extreme poverty,  
Śaṅkara sat down and composed this stotram—  
**22 verses of pure compassion**—  
invoking Goddess Mahā Lakṣmī to shower grace upon her.

Moved by the depth of his prayer,  
Lakṣmī immediately blessed the woman,  
and **golden āmla fruits** (kanaka + dhārā = golden shower)  
began falling into her home.

Thus this hymn became known as:

### 🌟 **Śrī Kanakadhārā Stotram**

_“The Hymn that Brings the Shower of Gold.”_

It is said that those who chant it with humility and devotion  
receive blessings of prosperity, relief from suffering,  
and the compassionate glance of the Goddess Herself.
