
🕉 **Śrī Veṅkaṭeśa Stotram — Verse 1**
======================================




🕉 **Verse 1 — कमलाकुचचूचुककुङ्कुमतो**
======================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

कमलाकुचचूचुककुङ्कुमतो  
नियतारुणितातुलनीलतनो ।  
कमलायतलोचन लोकपते  
विजयी भव वेङ्कटशैलपते ॥ १॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

kamalā-kuca-cūcuka-kuṅkuma-to  
niyatāruṇitātula-nīla-tano |  
kamalāyata-locana loka-pate  
vijayī bhava veṅkaṭa-śaila-pate || 1 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| कमला-कुच-चूचुक-कुङ्कुमतः | Whose divine body is tinged with the kumkum from Lakṣmī’s bosom |
| नियत-अरुणित | Ever glowing with a soft reddish radiance |
| अतुल-नील-तनः | Possessing an incomparable deep-blue form |
| कमला-अयत-लोचन | Lotus-eyed Lord |
| लोक-पते | Lord of the universe |
| विजयी भव | Be ever victorious |
| वेङ्कट-शैल-पते | O Lord of the Vēṅkaṭa Hills |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“Auspiciousness to the Lord of Vēṅkaṭa!  
His wondrous blue form glows with the sacred kumkum of Goddess Lakṣmī.  
His lotus eyes shine with compassion for all beings.  
May the Lord of Vēṅkaṭa ever remain victorious!”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. Lakṣmī’s Grace Made Visible**  
The kumkum from Mahālakṣmī symbolizes prosperity, purity, and divine affection.  
When it adorns the Lord’s form, it shows that **grace is inseparable from Him**.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Where He is, grace already flows.”

⭐ **2\. The Incomparable Blue Radiance (atula-nīlatana)**  
His deep-blue complexion reflects cosmic infinity—  
a reminder that the Divine is vast, timeless, and all-encompassing.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “The Infinite holds me gently.”

⭐ **3\. The Lotus Eyes That See All Without Judgment**  
Lotus eyes reflect compassion and clarity.  
They see our faults without rejection, and our strengths without pride.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May His gaze purify my mind.”

⭐ **4\. The Lord of Vēṅkaṭa as the Ever-Victorious One**  
Calling the Lord _vijayī bhava_ is a devotee’s prayer:  
“May righteousness and divine protection always prevail through You.”  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Victory is certain where the Lord resides.”

🕉 **Verse 2 — सचतुर्मुखषण्मुखपञ्चमुख**
=======================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

सचतुर्मुखषण्मुखपञ्चमुख-  
प्रमुखाखिलदैवतमौलिमणे ।  
शरणागतवत्सल सारनिधे  
परिपालय मां वृषशैलपते ॥ २॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

sa-catur-mukha-ṣaṇ-mukha-pañca-mukha-  
pramukhākhila-daivata-mauli-maṇe |  
śaraṇāgata-vatsala sāra-nidhe  
paripālaya māṁ vṛṣa-śaila-pate || 2 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| स-चतुर्मुख | along with Brahmā (four-faced) |
| षण्मुख | and Subrahmaṇya (six-faced) |
| पञ्चमुख | and Śiva in His five-faced form |
| प्रमुख-अखिल-दैवतः | foremost among all the gods |
| मौलि-मणे | the crown jewel upon their heads |
| शरणागत-व्त्सल | tenderly compassionate toward those who surrender |
| सार-निधे | treasure-house of all virtues and essence |
| परिपालय माम् | protect me; preserve me |
| वृष-शैल-पते | O Lord of Vṛṣabhādri (Tirumala) |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“O Lord of Vṛṣaśaila!  
You are the supreme jewel adorning the heads of Brahmā, Subrahmaṇya, and Śiva.  
You are the ocean of compassion for those who seek refuge.  
Please protect me always, O compassionate treasure of divine essence.”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. The Lord Above All Deities**  
When the hymn says He is the _mauli-maṇi_—the jewel on the crown of Brahmā, Śiva, and Subrahmaṇya—it means:  
The very gods who rule creation, preservation, and dissolution  
**bow to Him**.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “I surrender to the One whom even the gods revere.”

⭐ **2\. The Refuge-Giving Lord (śaraṇāgata-vatsala)**  
The Lord’s affection flows effortlessly toward those who surrender.  
His grace is not conditional—it is natural.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Surrender is the doorway to divine protection.”

⭐ **3\. The Ocean of the Essence (sāra-nidhiḥ)**  
All virtues—patience, strength, kindness, knowledge, purity—  
find their _purest form_ in Him.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May His qualities reflect in my heart.”

⭐ **4\. The Plea: Protect Me (paripālaya mām)**  
This is not a request of weakness,  
but a devotional recognition that **true protection comes only from the Divine**.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Under His shelter, I am unshaken.”

🕉 **Verse 3 — अतिवेलतया तव दुर्विषहैः**
========================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

अतिवेलतया तव दुर्विषहैः  
अनुवेलकृतैरपराधशतैः ।  
भरितं त्वरितं वृषशैलपते  
परया कृपया परिपाहि हरे ॥ ३॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

ativēlatayā tava durviṣahaiḥ  
anuvēla-kṛtair aparādha-śataiḥ |  
bhritaṁ tvaritaṁ vṛṣa-śaila-pate  
parayā kṛpayā paripāhi hare || 3 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अतिवेलतया | by excessive negligence; by great carelessness |
| तव दुर्विषहैः | toward You, with unbearable (offensive) conduct |
| अनुवेल-कृतैः | done repeatedly and continuously |
| अपराध-शतैः | hundreds of transgressions, faults |
| भरितम् | filled / burdened |
| त्वरितम् | quickly / urgently |
| वृष-शैल-पते | O Lord of Vṛṣaśaila (Tirumala) |
| परया कृपया | with supreme compassion |
| परिपाहि | protect; save |
| हरे | O Hari |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“O Lord of Vṛṣaśaila!  
Through negligence and repeated mistakes,  
I have committed countless offenses against You.  
Burdened by these faults, I seek Your swift mercy—  
O Hari, protect me with Your supreme compassion!”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. Honest Confession Before the Divine**  
This verse is the devotee’s humble admission:  
“We err constantly—not just once, but again and again.”  
Acknowledging this truth purifies the heart.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Awareness of my faults is the beginning of grace.”

⭐ **2\. The Lord’s Compassion Exceeds Our Imperfections**  
Even hundreds of mistakes do not diminish His readiness to forgive.  
His compassion is _parā_—beyond limits.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “His mercy is greater than my shortcomings.”

⭐ **3\. The Urgency of the Prayer (tvaritam)**  
The devotee feels the weight of accumulated wrongs  
and pleads for quick deliverance—  
not out of fear, but longing to return to purity.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “I turn toward Him without delay.”

⭐ **4\. Protection as the Lord’s Natural Response**  
When the devotee surrenders with sincerity,  
the Lord’s protection comes naturally, effortlessly.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “In surrender, I find sanctuary.”

🕉 **Verse 4 — अधिवेङ्कटशैलमुदारमतेः**
======================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

अधिवेङ्कटशैलमुदारमते-  
र्जनताभिमताधिकदानरतात् ।  
परदेवतया गदितान्निगमैः  
कमलादयितान्न परं कलये ॥ ४॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

adhivēṅkaṭa-śaila-mudāra-mater  
janatābhimata-adhika-dāna-ratāt |  
para-devatayā gaditān nigamaiḥ  
kamalā-dayitān na paraṁ kalayē || 4 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अधि-वॆङ्कट-शैलम् | upon the sacred Vēṅkaṭa Hill |
| उदार-मतेः | of noble and generous nature |
| जनता-अभिमत | beloved by the people; fulfilling their desires |
| अधिक-दान-रतात् | delighted in giving abundantly |
| पर-देवतया | as the Supreme Deity |
| गदितान् | declared, proclaimed |
| निगमैः | by the scriptures |
| कमला-दयितान् | the beloved of Goddess Lakṣmī |
| न परम् कलये | I know none superior |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“I know no deity higher than the beloved of Lakṣmī—  
the generous Lord who dwells upon Vēṅkaṭa Hill,  
who delights in granting more than what devotees desire,  
and whom the scriptures praise as the Supreme Lord.”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. The Lord of Vēṅkaṭa as the Generous Giver**  
He does not merely answer prayers—  
He grants _more_ than devotees even imagine (_adhika-dāna-ratāḥ_).  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “The Divine gives in ways far beyond my expectations.”

⭐ **2\. The People’s Beloved (janatābhimata)**  
This highlights His closeness to His devotees.  
He is not distant or demanding—He is accessible and adored.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “I am seen. I am heard. I am loved.”

⭐ **3\. Scriptures Declare Him Supreme**  
_Nigamaiḥ gaditān_ means that the Vedas themselves proclaim  
that no deity surpasses Lakṣmī’s beloved, Śrīnivāsa.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “When the Vedas point to Him, I too turn to Him.”

⭐ **4\. The Devotee’s Firm Resolution**  
_na paraṁ kalayē_ — “I know no other.”  
This is the essence of surrender:  
a heart that recognizes one ultimate refuge.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May my devotion be single-pointed.”

🕉 **Verse 5 — कलवेणुरवावशगोपवधू**
==================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

कलवेणुरवावशगोपवधू-  
शतकोटिवृतात्स्मरकोटिसमात् ।  
प्रतिवल्लविकाभिमतात्सुखदात्  
वसुदेवसुतान्न परं कलये ॥ ५॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

kala-vēṇu-ravāvaśa-gopa-vadhū-  
śata-koṭi-vṛtāt smara-koṭi-samāt |  
prati-vallavikābhimatāt sukha-dāt  
vasudēva-sutān na paraṁ kalayē || 5 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| कल-वॆणु-रव | the sweet sound of the flute |
| आवश | captivating, enchanting |
| गोप-वधू | the cowherd maidens (gopīs) |
| शत-कोटि-वृतात् | surrounded by millions of devotees |
| स्मर-कोटि-समात् | equal to millions of Cupids in beauty |
| प्रति-वल्लविका-अभिमत | beloved by every gopī; the cherished one |
| सुख-दात् | giver of joy |
| वसुदेव-सुतात् | the son of Vasudeva (Krishna) |
| न परम् कलये | I know none higher |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“I know no one superior to Krishna—  
the son of Vasudeva—  
whose flute captivates millions of gopīs,  
whose beauty surpasses countless Cupids,  
and who is the beloved of every devotee  
and the giver of supreme joy.”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. The Flute as the Call of the Divine**  
The soft music of Krishna’s flute symbolizes  
the subtle, irresistible pull of divine love.  
It awakens longing in the devotee’s heart.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May I hear His call even in silence.”

⭐ **2\. Beauty Beyond Measure**  
_smara-koṭi-samāt_ — “equal to millions of Cupids.”  
This describes the irresistible charm of the Divine:  
not merely physical beauty but **the beauty of pure consciousness**.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “The Divine shines in incomparable loveliness.”

⭐ **3\. Beloved of Every Gopī**  
Each gopī represents a soul longing for God.  
To each, Krishna appears uniquely personal, intimate.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “He relates to every soul in a way tailored to its heart.”

⭐ **4\. The Giver of Joy (sukhada)**  
The ultimate joy is not sensory pleasure,  
but the bliss of resting in God’s presence.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let my happiness arise from Him alone.”

🕉 **Verse 6 — अभिरामगुणाकर दाशरथे**
====================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

अभिरामगुणाकर दाशरथे  
जगदेकधनुर्धर धीरमते ।  
रघुनायक राम रमेश विभो  
वरदो भव देव दयाजलधे ॥ ६॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

abhirāma-guṇākara dāśarathē  
jagad-ēka-dhanurdhara dhīra-matē |  
raghu-nāyaka rāma ramēśa vibhō  
varadō bhava dēva dayā-jaladhē || 6 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अभिराम-गुण-आकर | mine of charming and noble qualities |
| दाशरथे | O son of Daśaratha (Rāma) |
| जगत्-एक-धनुः-धर | the peerless wielder of the bow in all the worlds |
| धीर-मते | of steadfast and noble mind |
| रघु-नायक | leader of the Raghu dynasty |
| राम | O Rāma |
| रमेश | Lord of Lakṣmī |
| विभो | O all-pervading One |
| वरदः भव | be the bestower of boons |
| देव | O divine Lord |
| दया-जलधे | ocean of compassion |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“O Rāma, treasure-house of delightful virtues,  
son of Daśaratha, and unrivalled wielder of the bow!  
O noble-minded Lord of the Raghu lineage,  
husband of Lakṣmī and all-pervading One—  
O ocean of mercy, Divine Lord—please grant Your blessings!”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. Rāma as the Embodiment of Noble Qualities**  
_abhirāma-guṇākara_ praises Him as the very **mine of virtues**—  
courage, compassion, truthfulness, humility.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May I reflect even a fraction of His noble qualities.”

⭐ **2\. The Unmatched Warrior (jagat-eka-dhanurdhara)**  
Rāma symbolizes righteous strength—  
power used only for dharma, never for ego.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let my strength serve righteousness alone.”

⭐ **3\. The Husband of Lakṣmī (rameśa)**  
Where Rāma is, Lakṣmī is—  
meaning where dharma is, prosperity naturally follows.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May my life invite divine harmony.”

⭐ **4\. Ocean of Compassion (dayā-jaladhi)**  
This title assures devotees that  
**no one is beyond His forgiveness**.  
His compassion is vast, deep, and inexhaustible.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “I rest in His boundless mercy.”

🕉 **Verse 7 — अवनीतनयाकमनीयकरं**
=================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

अवनीतनयाकमनीयकरं  
रजनीकरचारुमुखाम्बुरुहम् ।  
रजनीचरराजतमोमिहिरं  
महनीयमहं रघुराममये ॥ ७॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

avanī-tanaya-kamanīya-karaṁ  
rajanī-kara-cāru-mukhāmburuham |  
rajanī-cara-rāja-tamo-mihiraṁ  
mahanīyam ahaṁ raghu-rāmam ayē || 7 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अवनी-तनय | Sītā, the daughter of the earth |
| कमनीय-करम् | one who delights her; pleasing and graceful |
| रजनी-कर | the moon |
| चारु-मुख-अम्बुरुहम् | whose lotus face is charming like moonlight |
| रजनी-चर-राज | the king of night-wanderers (Rāvaṇa) |
| तमः-मिहिरम् | the sun who destroys darkness (Rāma as destroyer of evil) |
| महनीयम् | the exalted one |
| अहम् | I (the devotee) |
| रघु-रामम् | Rāma of the Raghu lineage |
| अये | revere, adore, worship |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“I adore Rāma, the exalted scion of the Raghu race—  
whose presence delights Sītā,  
whose lotus-like face shines with the beauty of moonlight,  
and who, like the rising sun, destroys  
the darkness embodied in the king of night-wanderers (Rāvaṇa).”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. Rāma as the Joy of Sītā**  
Calling Him _kamanīya-kara_ highlights the perfect harmony between Rāma and Sītā—  
He is the presence that brings peace, joy, and reassurance to Her.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Divine love manifests as mutual upliftment.”

⭐ **2\. The Moon-faced Lord**  
His face, compared to the moon’s cool radiance,  
soothes the mind and calms emotional storms.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May His serene beauty settle my heart.”

⭐ **3\. The Sun That Dispels Darkness**  
As _tamo-mihira_, Rāma destroys the darkness of ignorance, ego, and adharma—  
just as sunlight dispels night instantly.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let His light dissolve my inner shadows.”

⭐ **4\. The Devotee’s Declaration of Faith**  
_mahanīyam ahaṁ raghu-rāmam ayē_ means:  
“I hold Rāma as the supreme object of devotion.”  
This is the heart declaring its eternal anchor.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “My devotion is rooted in righteousness and love.”

🕉 **Verse 8 — सुमुखं सुहृदं सुलभं सुखदं**
==========================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

सुमुखं सुहृदं सुलभं सुखदं

sumukhaṁ suhṛdaṁ sulabhaṁ sukhadaṁ

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| सुमुखम् | of beautiful, auspicious face |
| सुहृदम् | the true well-wisher; ever-loving friend |
| सुलभम् | easily accessible to devotees |
| सुखदम् | giver of happiness and peace |
| स्व-अनुजम् | accompanied by His dear brother (Lakṣmaṇa) |
| सु-कायम् | of noble, excellent form |
| अमोघ-शरम् | whose arrows never fail; infallible |
| अपहाय | abandoning, giving up |

| अन्यम् | any other deity |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“I shall never worship anyone other than Rāma—  
the beautiful and compassionate Lord,  
easily accessible to all, the giver of joy,  
noble in form and accompanied by Lakṣmaṇa,  
and whose arrows are unfailing in protecting His devotees.”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. The Accessible Lord (sulabha)**  
This verse emphasizes that the Divine is not distant.  
Rāma is approachable, relatable, and near to the devotee’s heart.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “The Divine is never far from those who seek sincerely.”

⭐ **2\. The True Well-Wisher (suhṛd)**  
Rāma’s love is not transactional—  
He cares for the devotee’s growth, peace, and liberation.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let me trust the One who always wants my highest good.”

⭐ **3\. The Infallible Protector (amogha-śara)**  
His arrows never miss—symbolizing  
the precision with which divine grace destroys ignorance, fear, and obstacles.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “His protection never fails.”

⭐ **4\. Exclusive Devotion (ekāntabhakti)**  
The devotee here makes a firm vow:  
“I will not turn to anyone else—my heart rests only in Rāma.”  
This is the pinnacle of surrender.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let my devotion remain steady and undivided.”

vinā veṅkaṭēśaṁ na nāthō na nāthaḥ  
sadā veṅkaṭēśaṁ smarāmi smarāmi |  
harē veṅkaṭēśa prasīda prasīda  
priyaṁ veṅkaṭēśa prayaccha prayaccha || 9 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| विना | without |
| वेङ्कटेशम् | Lord Vēṅkaṭeśa |
| न नाथः | no protector |
| न नाथः | no master / guardian |
| सदा | always, constantly |
| स्मरामि | I remember, I meditate upon |
| हरे | O Hari |
| प्रसीद | be gracious, show Your mercy |
| प्रियं | what is dear to me / what I seek |
| प्रयच्छ | please grant, bestow |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“Without Lord Vēṅkaṭeśa, there is no protector, no refuge.  
I constantly remember Him—again and again.  
O Hari, O Vēṅkaṭeśa—be merciful!  
Please grant me that which is dear and auspicious.”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. Declaring the Sole Refuge**  
_vinā veṅkaṭēśaṁ na nāthaḥ_  
This is a profound statement of surrender:  
“No protector exists for me apart from Vēṅkaṭeśa.”  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “I rest all burdens in His hands.”

⭐ **2\. Repeated Remembrance (smarāmi smarāmi)**  
The repetition shows a heart overflowing with devotion.  
True remembrance is continuous—not occasional.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May my mind return to Him again and again.”

⭐ **3\. The Plea for Grace (prasīda prasīda)**  
Devotion ripens into longing for divine mercy.  
The soul knows only the Lord can soften suffering and guide destiny.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let His grace fill every part of my life.”

⭐ **4\. Asking for What Is Truly Dear (priyaṁ prayaccha)**  
The devotee does not ask for wealth or power—  
only for what is **truly** blessed and uplifting.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May I desire only what leads me closer to Him.”

🕉 **Verse 10 — अहं दूरतस्ते पदाम्भोजयुग्म**
============================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

अहं दूरतस्ते पदाम्भोजयुग्म-  
प्रणामेच्छयाऽऽगत्य सेवां करोमि ।  
सकृत्सेवया नित्यसेवाफलं त्वं  
प्रयच्छ प्रयच्छ प्रभो वेङ्कटेश ॥ १०॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

ahaṁ dūratas te padāmbhoja-yugma-  
praṇāmecchayā ’āgatya sevāṁ karōmi |  
sakrit-sēvayā nitya-sēvā-phalaṁ tvaṁ  
prayaccha prayaccha prabhō veṅkaṭēśa || 10 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अहम् | I |
| दूरतः | from afar |
| ते | Your |
| पद-अम्भोज-युग्म | pair of lotus feet |
| प्रणाम-इच्छया | with the desire to prostrate |
| आगत्य | having come |
| सेवाम् करोमि | I perform service, I worship |
| सकृत्-सेवया | by serving even once |
| नित्य-सेवा-फलम् | the fruit of eternal service |
| त्वम् | You |
| प्रयच्छ | please grant |
| प्रभो | O Lord |
| वेङ्कटेश | O Vēṅkaṭeśa |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“Though I am far away, I come to Your lotus feet  
with the desire to bow and serve You.  
By even a single act of service,  
please grant me the merit of serving You eternally—  
O Lord Vēṅkaṭeśa, please bestow this blessing!”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. Coming From Afar (dūratas)**  
Distance here is not physical but spiritual—  
the soul feels separated due to ignorance or past karma.  
Yet the moment we _desire_ Him, we “come near.”  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “No distance is too great when the heart turns toward Him.”

⭐ **2\. The Desire to Bow (praṇāma-icchā)**  
The first step in devotion is a sincere longing to surrender.  
This longing itself purifies the mind.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let humility flower within me.”

⭐ **3\. The Power of a Single Act of Service**  
_sakrit-sevayā_ — “even one service.”  
Just one sincere act of devotion  
can carry the potency of countless lifetimes.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “A moment of genuine devotion transforms destiny.”

⭐ **4\. Asking for Eternal Service (nitya-sevā-phalam)**  
The devotee does not seek material gain—  
but the highest spiritual blessing:  
**the opportunity to serve forever.**  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “May my life always remain in His service.”

🕉 **Verse 11 — अज्ञानिना मया दोषान्**
======================================

🔱 Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)
------------------------

अज्ञानिना मया दोषान्  
अशेषान् विहितान् हरे ।  
क्षमस्व त्वं क्षमस्व त्वं  
शेषशैलशिखामणे ॥ ११॥

* * *

🔤 Transliteration
------------------

ajñāninā mayā dōṣān  
aśēṣān vihitān harē |  
kṣamasva tvaṁ kṣamasva tvaṁ  
śeṣa-śaila-śikhā-maṇē || 11 ||

* * *

🧩 Padaccheda & Word-by-word Meaning
------------------------------------

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| अज्ञानिना | by me, who is ignorant |
| मया | by me |
| दोषान् | faults, wrongdoings |
| अशेषान् | without remainder; countless |
| विहितान् | committed, done |
| हरे | O Hari |
| क्षमस्व त्वम् | please forgive me |
| शेष-शैल-शिखा-मणे | jewel adorning the peak of Śeṣa Mountain (Vēṅkaṭa Hill) |

* * *

🌿 Simple Translation
---------------------

“O Hari!  
Out of ignorance I have committed countless faults.  
Please forgive me—  
O shining jewel upon the peak of Śeṣa Mountain!”

* * *

🧘 Meditative Insight
---------------------

⭐ **1\. The Humility of True Devotion**  
The devotee openly admits:  
“All my mistakes arise from ignorance.”  
Such humility purifies the heart more than austerities.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Awareness of my limitations opens me to grace.”

⭐ **2\. Countless Faults, Infinite Forgiveness**  
No one can enumerate their faults—  
but the Lord’s compassion exceeds them all.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Divine mercy is greater than my shortcomings.”

⭐ **3\. Repetition of ‘Forgive Me’ (kṣamasva tvaṁ)**  
Repetition expresses deep longing.  
The soul cries twice because forgiveness is its lifeline.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “Let my heart soften with each prayer.”

⭐ **4\. Jewel of Śeṣa Hill**  
Calling Him the _jewel_ on the summit of Vēṅkaṭa  
reflects His unmatched splendor and His role as the protector of all.  
🕯️ _Meditation:_ “In His radiance, all darkness dissolves.”
