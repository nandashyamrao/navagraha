O Treasure of Grace, O Mountain of Bliss!

<p><strong>Meditative meaning</strong><br/>
Grace is called a “treasure” because it cannot be earned like wages; it is gifted.  
Bliss is not a passing pleasure but a solid “mountain” in Him.  
Compassion flows endlessly like the waves of an ocean.  
When we pray “palli ezhundharuḷāy”, we are really asking:  
“Lord, let this grace, bliss, and compassion fully awaken in my own heart.”
